01 — Who

Raised in Switzerland, rooted in Kuwait, building in Boston.

I'm a computer science student at Northeastern who never picked a single lane. I write code, edit video, run cross country, help run a finance society, and chase down a good story — and I've found the most interesting work lives where those worlds overlap.

I founded Yousefully back in 2023, built this site from scratch during a sprint at Liip, got an inside look at product, finance, and ops at Thomson Reuters, and drove technology initiatives at Hebbian. I spent my first year with Northeastern's NYC Scholars program — serving as Secretary of the NYC Finance Society — and I'm now based in Boston. I care about craft — the difference a custom cursor or a well-timed animation makes.
